HIDAMES LUDO RULES

Official Ludo King Tournament Standards

🎲 Token Release

A pawn can leave base only on rolling a 6. Rolling a 6 grants an immediate extra roll.

🚫 Three Consecutive Sixes

Rolling three consecutive 6s in one turn cancels all movement from that sequence, passing the turn immediately.

⚔️ Captures & Knockouts

Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to base and awards the capturing player a bonus roll.

⭐ Safe Squares

The 4 colored starting squares and 4 star-marked squares are 100% safe. Tokens on these cannot be captured.

🛡️ Blockades

Two tokens of the same color on a non-safe cell form a blockade. Opponents cannot pass through or land on it.

🎯 Exact Finish

Pawns must complete the outer track, enter their home lane, and reach the center goal with an exact dice roll.

🤝 Team Matches

In 2v2 or team modes, teammates cannot capture each other. The first team to get all members' tokens home wins!
